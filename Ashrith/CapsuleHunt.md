# Capsule: Hunt the Hidden Trigger

**Category:** AI/ML · Adversarial Machine Learning  
**Difficulty:** Medium–Hard

Something is wrong with Capsule. Its black-box classifier predicts **APPROVE** or **REJECT**, but certain inputs make it behave unexpectedly.

Your mission is to investigate its predictions, analyze confidence scores, and uncover the hidden trigger buried in its decision space.

You have **2,500 queries** and limited submission attempts. The trigger location is unique to each team.

**Map the boundary. Find the anomaly. Recover the flag.**
