The Gradient Gambit

Category: AI/ML · Adversarial Machine Learning
Difficulty: Medium–Hard

Capsule's component classifier seems reliable. Feed it an input, and it predicts whether the component is a Resistor, Capacitor, or Inductor.

But beneath its quantized decision space lies a weakness waiting to be discovered.

Your mission is to probe its predictions, exploit its decision boundaries, and craft an input that makes the model see what it shouldn't. Clearing the first inspection is only the beginning: the final stage introduces unpredictable jitter, putting the robustness of your attack to the test.

Can you fool the classifier twice and survive the final inspection?

Break the boundary. Beat the jitter. Recover the flag.
